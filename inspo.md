# Design Inspiration & Editorial Standards (inspo.md)

> **Design Tenet**: A human-crafted, architectural software engineering portfolio built on **crisp neutrals**, **warm earthy tones**, and **intentional soft & bold contrasts**. Feels designed and refined by human hands—deliberate, tactile, thoughtful, and authentic—stripping away all synthetic "vibe-coded" AI aesthetics.

---

## 2. "Human-Made" Visual Principles

1. **Editorial Architecture**:
   - Numbered sections with serif Roman numerals or clean technical indices (`01 / SELECTED WORK`, `02 / BLUEPRINT`, `03 / CRAFT & STACK`, `04 / NOTES`).
   - Architectural grid layout reminiscent of Swiss graphic design and architectural monographs.
2. **Tactile & Grounded, Not Synthetic**:
   - No generic neon glows or purple-pink gradients.
   - Solid, tactile surfaces with warm hairline borders and authentic paper-like depth.
3. **Voice & Substance**:
   - Natural, human engineering commentary ("Notes from the workbench", "Why this architectural tradeoff was chosen").
   - Clear problem definitions, real-world CVE remediations, and verifiable benchmarks.
4. **Accessible Craftsmanship**:
   - Full keyboard navigation with visible warm terracotta focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-clay-400`).
   - High contrast meeting WCAG 2.1 AA across all states.
   - Semantic HTML with labeled inputs and touch targets `>= 44px`.
